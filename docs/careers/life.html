Life at Forster and Partners

A closer look at the people, pace, and culture behind the work we do.

← Back to Careers

We're a small firm by design. That means less hierarchy and more hands-on experience — you'll work directly with partners and clients from early on, rather than being buried on a large engagement team. It also means the people you sit next to are the people who show up for you, year after year.

🤝

Mentorship

Work directly alongside experienced partners and senior staff who are invested in your professional development.

📈

Growth

Gain broad exposure across industries and service lines, from audit and tax to advisory work, as you build your skills.

🏢

Culture

Join a collegial, close-knit team that takes pride in long-term relationships with clients and with each other.

A Day in the Life

No two days look exactly the same, but a few things are constant.

👥

Real Client Contact

You're not shielded from clients until year five. Staff at every level join client calls, sit in on planning meetings, and see how advice translates into decisions.

🎓

Learning on the Job

Partners and managers work alongside you, not just above you. Questions get answered in person, and feedback comes while the work is still fresh.

🗂️

Varied Engagements

Because our clients span construction, real estate, tech, and not-for-profits, the work rarely feels repetitive — you build breadth as well as depth.

What You Can Expect

We invest in the people who invest in our clients. That shows up in how engagements are staffed, how feedback is given, and how careers are built here over time.

Working Here Means

  • ✓  Direct access to partners, not layers of management
  • ✓  Support toward your CPA designation and continued education
  • ✓  Exposure across audit, tax, and advisory work
  • ✓  A collegial, close-knit office rather than a large-firm environment
  • ✓  Long-term client relationships you get to be part of

What Guides Us

The values that shape how we work, together and with clients.

Integrity

We give the advice we'd want to receive, even when it isn't the easy answer.

Mentorship

Experience is meant to be shared, not guarded — we grow by teaching each other.

Long-Term Thinking

Whether it's a client relationship or a career here, we build for the long run.
