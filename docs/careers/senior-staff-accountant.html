Senior Staff Accountant

Join our team and grow your career in public accounting.

← Back to Careers

Forster and Partners LLP is looking for a Senior Staff Accountant to join our team. This is an opportunity to work closely with partners and clients across audit, tax, and advisory engagements, while continuing to build your technical skills and career in public accounting.

About the Role

As a Senior Staff Accountant, you will take a lead role on client engagements, working directly with partners and mentoring junior staff. You'll gain broad exposure across industries including construction, real estate, technology, and not-for-profits.

Responsibilities

  • •  Plan and execute audit, review, and compilation engagements
  • •  Prepare and review corporate, personal, and trust tax returns
  • •  Supervise and mentor junior staff on engagements
  • •  Communicate directly with clients throughout the engagement
  • •  Identify planning opportunities and technical issues for partner review

Qualifications

  • •  CPA designation (or nearing completion), with a solid foundation in Canadian accounting and tax standards
  • •  3+ years of public accounting experience, with exposure to audit, review, and tax engagements
  • •  Strong communication skills and a genuine interest in building long-term client relationships

How to Apply

If you are qualified and interested in applying for this position, please send your resume to: oananieva@fsscpa.ca
